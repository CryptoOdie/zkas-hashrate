Zcash

The proof system ZKAS is built on. Strong privacy, but optional, and much of the chain still runs in the open.

Monero

Start merged mining

  1. Keep your Kaspa setupSame ASIC, same firmware. Nothing changes on the machine.
  2. Point it at a merged-mining poolFor example mining-pool.zkas.info, with your ZKAS address and your KAS payout address.
  3. Earn both, pay for oneKaspa rewards as before, ZKAS rewards on top. Your power bill doesn't change.
