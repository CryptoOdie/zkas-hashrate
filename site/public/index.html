ZKAS price, in dollars

ZKAS was mined for four weeks before anyone could buy it. The price starts at the first trade on 23 August: the OTC desk before ZKAS was listed, then NonKYC and Neoxa, then CoinGecko.

ZKAS / USD

Until exchanges listed ZKAS on 14 September, the price is the community OTC desk's daily average, quoted in KAS and converted at that day's KAS price. From 28 September it is CoinGecko's price across exchanges.
